Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Babek ("the App", "we") collects, uses and protects information when you connect your Facebook or Instagram account to it.

1. What the App does

Babek is a private publishing tool. It lets its operator connect Facebook Pages and Instagram Business or Creator accounts they manage, prepare news posts, review them in Telegram, and publish them to those accounts. The App is not offered to the general public.

2. Information we collect

When you log in with Facebook and grant permissions, the App receives and stores only what it needs to publish on your behalf:

  • Your Facebook user ID and an access token issued by Meta.
  • The IDs, names and access tokens of the Facebook Pages you choose to connect.
  • The IDs and usernames of Instagram accounts linked to those Pages.
  • Basic account statistics (such as follower counts and post views) provided by the Instagram and Facebook APIs, used only to show performance to the operator.
  • The content of posts created and published through the App (text, images and videos).

The App does not collect your password, your private messages, your friends list, or any information about people who visit or follow your Pages.

3. How we use the information

  • To publish posts, images and videos to the Pages and Instagram accounts you connected.
  • To show you the status of scheduled and published posts.
  • To show basic performance statistics for your connected accounts.

We do not sell, rent or share your information for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Service providers

To work, the App uses the following third-party services:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram APIs) to publish content and read statistics.
  • Telegram to send post previews and notifications to the operator.
  • OpenAI to help write post titles and captions from public news articles. No Facebook or Instagram account data is sent to OpenAI.
  • Cloudflare R2 to temporarily host images and videos so Instagram can fetch them during publishing.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored in a private database controlled by the operator. Access tokens are encrypted before they are stored. Access to the database is restricted to the operator.

6. Data retention

Access tokens are kept only while your account is connected. When you disconnect the App or ask us to delete your data, your tokens and account information are deleted within 30 days.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You can remove the App's access at any time from your Facebook settings, and you can ask us to delete all data related to your account. See the Data Deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.

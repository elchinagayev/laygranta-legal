User Data Deletion

Last updated: October 30, 2026

You can remove Babek's access to your Facebook and Instagram accounts and ask us to delete all data we hold about you at any time.

Option 1: Remove the App from Facebook

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites (also found under Security → Business integrations).
  3. Find Babek and click Remove.

After removal, the App can no longer access your Pages or Instagram accounts, and the stored access tokens stop working.

Option 2: Ask us to delete your data

Send an email to elchinagayev06@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and include the name of your Facebook account or Page.

What is deleted

  • Your Facebook user ID and access tokens.
  • Connected Page and Instagram account IDs, names and tokens.
  • Stored statistics and post records related to your accounts.

Posts that were already published to your Pages or Instagram accounts stay there. You can delete them directly on Facebook or Instagram.

How long it takes

We delete your data within 30 days of receiving your request and confirm by email once it is done.
